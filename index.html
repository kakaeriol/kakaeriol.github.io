About Me

I'm a Ph.D. candidate at the National University of Singapore, advised by Professor Bryan Kian Hsiang Low, working at the intersection of data-centric machine learning and large-scale systems. My research focuses on data valuation, data selection, and uncertainty-aware modeling to make training large models more efficient and reliable.

I have a strong foundation in distributed systems, including two years of research on scheduling and optimization for HPC cluster workloads during my Master's at UNIST. I also bring 3+ years of industry experience building scalable, end-to-end ML systems in fintech, including production pipelines for credit scoring and large-scale data processing.

Research Focus

Data Valuation & Data-Centric Learning

Scalable approximations of Shapley values and influence estimation for efficient data valuation in large-scale model training, with a focus on uncertainty-aware utility prediction.

Data Selection for LLM Pretraining

Shapley-guided data selection and sampling-fraction allocation to optimize large-scale data mixtures under compute constraints.

Collaborative & Data Marketplace ML

Incentive mechanisms, fairness, and robust utility estimation in multi-agent data sharing and collaborative learning.

Scalable, Theory-Driven ML Systems

Turning theoretical models — game-theoretic valuation, uncertainty estimation — into production-scale pipelines and distributed training workflows (Spark, PySpark, SQL).

Research in Progress

  • Modality Value Estimation for Vision-Language Models

    A modality valuation framework that quantifies the marginal and interaction contributions of vision and language at the data-point level, guiding synthetic data generation and targeted augmentation for more robust multimodal models.

  • Shapley-Guided Interaction-Aware Data Selection for LLM Pretraining

    Model-aware data selection using in-run approximations of Data Shapley values that capture model state and within-batch interactions, enabling a lightweight scorer to dynamically rank large pretraining corpora for curriculum-style data curation.

  • Dynamic Data Valuation for Collaborative Retrieval Systems (GraphRAG)

    A graph-based framework that models retrieved evidence as a time-evolving graph and estimates node- and provider-level contributions with Shapley-based methods, enabling fair attribution in multi-provider RAG systems.
