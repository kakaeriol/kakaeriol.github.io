Welcome!

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the National University of Singapore, advised by Professor Bryan Kian Hsiang Low in the Group of Learning and Optimization Working in AI. I work at the intersection of data-centric machine learning and large-scale systems, focusing on data valuation, data selection, and uncertainty-aware modeling to make training large models more efficient and reliable.

Before my Ph.D., I spent two years on scheduling and optimization for HPC cluster workloads during my Master's at UNIST (Korea), and 3+ years in industry building scalable, end-to-end ML systems in fintech and edtech, including production pipelines for credit scoring and large-scale data processing.

My research interests include

  • Data valuation & data-centric learning (Shapley values, influence estimation)
  • Data selection and mixture optimization for LLM pretraining
  • Collaborative ML, data sharing and data marketplaces (incentives, fairness)

Research in Progress

  • Shapley-Guided Interaction-Aware Data Selection for LLM Pretraining

    Model-aware data selection using in-run approximations of Data Shapley values that capture both model state and within-batch interactions, enabling a lightweight scorer to dynamically rank large pretraining corpora for curriculum-style data curation under compute constraints.

  • Modality Value Estimation for Vision-Language Models

    A modality valuation framework that quantifies the marginal and interaction contributions of vision and language at the data-point level. These signals guide synthetic data generation and targeted augmentation, improving robustness and downstream performance of multimodal models.

  • Dynamic Data Valuation for Collaborative Retrieval Systems (GraphRAG)

    A graph-based framework that models retrieved evidence as a time-evolving graph and estimates node- and provider-level contributions with Shapley-based methods, enabling fair attribution and credit assignment in multi-provider RAG systems.
